COMPANY: BluePeak Software
YEAR: 2023

[PAGE 3]
BluePeak Software provides cloud-based treasury and cash management software, sold on a
subscription basis to mid-market and enterprise customers in Northern Europe and the DACH
region. During 2023 the group served 1,190 customers and employed 980 people across offices
in Stockholm, Amsterdam and Kraków; the Frankfurt office opened in January 2024. The
product was delivered from a mixture of multi-tenant and legacy single-tenant environments,
with 78 percent of customers on the multi-tenant platform at year end. Approximately 81
percent of revenue was subscription revenue.

[PAGE 7]
Revenue increased to EUR 540 million in 2023 from EUR 471 million, growth of 14.6 percent.
Growth was led by the Advanced edition and by initial traction in the DACH region following
the appointment of a regional sales leader. Subscription revenue grew 16.1 percent while
services revenue grew 5 percent. Deferred revenue at year end was EUR 231 million. The group
noted a lengthening of enterprise sales cycles during the second half, which it attributed
to increased procurement scrutiny of software budgets.

[PAGE 10]
Annual recurring revenue was EUR 462 million at 31 December 2023, up from EUR 398 million.
Net revenue retention was 111 percent and gross revenue retention was 95 percent. Average
annual recurring revenue per customer was EUR 361,000. The customer acquisition cost payback
period was 24 months. Management identified reduction of the payback period as a priority
for 2024, with actions focused on partner-sourced pipeline and a reduction in paid
acquisition spend.

[PAGE 13]
Adjusted EBITDA was EUR 108 million and adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.0 percent, compared
with 17.4 percent in 2022. Margin expansion reflected operating leverage on subscription
revenue growth, partially offset by elevated customer acquisition costs and by the cost of
operating two delivery environments during the platform migration. Sales and marketing
expense was 31 percent of revenue. Research and development expense was EUR 99 million,
equivalent to 18.3 percent of revenue. Cloud infrastructure expense was EUR 52 million,
spread across two providers.

[PAGE 16]
For 2024 management guided to revenue growth of 12 to 16 percent and an adjusted EBITDA
margin of 21 to 22 percent. The margin improvement was expected to come from lower customer
acquisition costs and from completing the migration of single-tenant customers, which would
remove the cost of running duplicate environments. Management stated an intention to
consolidate European cloud workloads with a single provider during 2024 in order to secure
committed-use discounts.

[PAGE 21]
Principal risks were competition from banking incumbents, dependence on two cloud
infrastructure providers, information security obligations relating to customer payment
data, lengthening enterprise sales cycles, and engineering recruitment in a competitive
labour market. The group held ISO 27001 certification and was preparing for a SOC 2 Type II
examination in the following year.
